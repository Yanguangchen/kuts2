Hair & nail services

Your hair and nails are part of your everyday confidence. We treat every cut, colour, perm and mani-pedi with the same care we would want for ourselves — clear communication first, then precise work.

  • Experienced stylists and nail technicians who stay current with techniques and products suited to Asian hair and nails.
  • Transparent menu pricing so you can decide before we begin — no surprise add-ons unless you ask for them.
  • Hygiene you can see: sanitised tools, disposable items where appropriate, and tidy stations between clients.
  • Honest recommendations — we suggest what fits your hair condition, lifestyle and budget, not the most expensive package.

Beauty add-ons

Lashes, brows, waxing and IPL sit close to your skin and your comfort zone. We prioritise safety, privacy and predictable results — so you leave feeling looked after, not rushed.

  • Trained application for lashes and brows, with attention to natural shape, symmetry and aftercare you can follow at home.
  • Waxing performed with care for skin type and sensitivity; we work in a calm, professional manner.
  • IPL sessions explained in plain language — what to expect, how many visits may be typical, and how we protect your comfort during treatment.
  • Discreet service in our Roxy Square II location; questions welcome before, during and after your visit.
