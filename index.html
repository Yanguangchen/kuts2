What we focus on:

  • Haircuts for men, women and children — practical styles you can manage at home.
  • Hair colour, highlights and non-ammonia options, chosen to suit your hair condition and maintenance routine.
  • Wash, scalp care, treatments and keratin services for dry, frizzy or post-colour hair.
  • Manicures, pedicures, gel polish, extensions, soak-off and nail art.
  • Perms and rebonding — only after a hair-condition check.

Tell us what you have in mind and we'll suggest what is realistic for your hair and budget.

What you can expect:

  • Professional salon products selected for the service you book — we'll tell you what we are using.
  • Technique chosen for your hair condition and desired finish, not the highest-priced package.
  • A consultation before we start: we discuss expectations, price, time and what is realistic.
  • Lower-ammonia and gentler colour options when your hair condition needs them.

We explain the options clearly before starting so you know what to expect — including the price.

What our space is like:

  • Tidy, well-lit salon space with comfortable seating and proper wash stations.
  • Sanitised tools between clients; disposable items used where appropriate.
  • A calm pace — no rushing, no pressure to add services.
  • Drinks offered while you wait or during longer services.
  • Open daily 9:30am – 8:30pm; appointments recommended, walk-ins welcome when available.
