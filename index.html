About Kuts & Kurls

We're your friendly neighbourhood salon at Roxy Square II. Our experienced stylists and nail artists deliver beautiful results with fair, transparent pricing. From precision haircuts, colour and perms to keratin and scalp care — plus classic and gel manicures, pedicures, lashes, brows, waxing and IPL — we make it easy to look and feel your best every day.

Our Specialties:

  • Professional haircuts and styling for all hair types
  • Advanced hair treatments and coloring techniques
  • Luxurious hair washing and scalp massages
  • Skilled nail technicians for manicures and pedicures
  • Perming services for stunning, long-lasting curls

With our experienced stylists, you can feel and look your best!

What Sets Us Apart:

  • Use of premium, salon-exclusive hair and nail products
  • Continuous training in the latest beauty trends and techniques
  • Personalized consultations for each client
  • Eco-friendly and cruelty-free product options available

At Kuts & Kurls, we combine skill with top-quality products to ensure you receive the best possible results for your hair and nails.

Your Comfort is Our Priority:

  • Modern, clean, and stylish salon space
  • Comfortable seating and wash stations
  • Soothing ambiance with calming music and aromatherapy
  • Complimentary beverages during your visit
  • Flexible scheduling options to fit your busy lifestyle

We strive to make every visit to Kuts & Kurls a pampering experience. Book your appointment today and discover the difference!
