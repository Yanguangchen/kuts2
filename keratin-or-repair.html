Hair guide · 26 October 2026

Keratin or a repair treatment

Kuts & Kurls lists both under hair treatments at 50 East Coast Road, #01-135, Roxy Square II. They are different lines and different prices. The salon asks you to consult before a chemical or treatment service is chosen. WhatsApp 9636 3537.

The published ranges

  • Repair hair treatment: $128 to $168.
  • Keratin hair treatment: $268 to $358.

Nearby lines, so you do not book the wrong name: migration hair treatment is $68 to $108, nano hair treatment is $228 to $288, and scalp treatment is $98 to $198. A range means the price is confirmed after the hair is seen. It is not a promise that every head pays the bottom number.

How to tell them apart when you message

Repair is the lower treatment range. Keratin is the higher one. The menu does not print a wear time for either, so this page will not invent one. Say what the hair is doing: dry after colour, frizz you want smoothed, or a scalp that needs its own visit. If the concern is the scalp, the scalp line is the one to name. If you want a straightening service, rebonding and digital perm are separate menus, not these two.
