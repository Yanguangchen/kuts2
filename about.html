About Kuts & Kurls

Clear pricing, careful consultations, and practical beauty services for East Coast customers who want good results without salon pressure.

Who we are

Kuts & Kurls is a hair and beauty salon at Roxy Square II offering haircuts, colour, perms, treatments, manicures, pedicures, lashes, brows, waxing and IPL.

Our focus is simple: clear pricing, careful consultations, and services that suit your everyday lifestyle. Before starting, we help you understand what works for your hair, skin, nails, budget and maintenance routine — so you can make a confident decision.

Whether you are coming in for a quick haircut, a full colour service, a perm or a nail appointment, our goal is to give you a comfortable visit and a result you can maintain after you leave.

How we work

Many customers come to us because they want a salon that explains the price clearly before starting. Whether it is a simple trim, a perm, colour or nail service, we focus on practical advice, honest recommendations, and results that suit your lifestyle.

If a service is not right for your hair condition or budget, we will say so. If a simpler option will give you the same result, we will recommend that instead.

What you can expect at our salon

  • A consultation before we start — we discuss expectations, time and the price.
  • Professional salon products selected for the service you book; we'll tell you what we are using.
  • A tidy, well-lit space with sanitised tools and disposable items where appropriate.
  • No hard selling. If an add-on is not necessary, we will not push it.
  • Aftercare advice you can actually follow — washing, drying and product use at home.
