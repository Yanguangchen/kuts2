Soft rebonding or a digital perm

Kuts & Kurls is at 50 East Coast Road, #01-135, Roxy Square II. Perms and rebonding are booked only after a hair-condition check. The two prices people compare most often are not interchangeable.

The published ranges

  • Soft rebonding: $152–$248. This is the smoother, straighter option on the menu.
  • Digital perm: $228–$288. This is the curl option.
  • Cool perm: $118–$158, if you want a curl at the lower end of the menu.
  • Steambond: $228–$328, and Korean nano perm: $248–$328, if the consult points somewhere else.

The range is there because length and condition change the price. The number inside the range is part of the consultation, not a figure you guess from a photo.

How to tell which sentence you mean

Ask for soft rebonding if you want the hair smoother and less wavy. Ask for a digital perm if you want a curl or wave to stay. A keratin or repair treatment is a different menu — keratin is $268–$358, repair treatment is $128–$168 — and it does not replace either chemical service. If the hair has been bleached, rebonded, or permed recently, say so before a date is held. The salon’s own line is that the method is chosen to match the curl you want and the condition the hair can take.
